Key takeaways

1
Best week since 27 July, and broad. +6.6% to £1.67m with 71 of 105 tracked brands up, a clean reversal of last week's 34 up / 71 down. Orders +4.2% and AOV +2.3%, so both levers contributed rather than one.
2
Core supplements drove it. Pure Encapsulations (+£15.2k), Thorne (+£9.4k) and Codeage (+£4.4k) added £29.0k, reversing the exact brands behind last week's fall. Designs for Health fully recovered its 26% drop, which supports the order-timing read.
3
Seed is reinstated. £1,278 to £6,818, so roughly £7k a week of total sales recovered after the short-expiry discontinuation was reversed. That was the largest single recoverable line on the discontinued-SKU list.
4
Tech did not participate. WHOOP, Garmin, COROS, Hyperice and Therabody fell £15.5k between them in a week when almost everything else rose. Worth checking whether the offer excluded tech SKUs, since that is where margin pressure usually sits.
5
Still below the July peak. £1.67m against £1.85m on 27 July, so 9.5% of the gap remains. LMNT reversing its gain and WHOOP down a third straight week are the two lines holding it back.
