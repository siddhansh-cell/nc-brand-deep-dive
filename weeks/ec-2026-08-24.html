Key takeaways

1
Best week in the window at £1.86m, and the broadest. 86 of 115 tracked brands up against 29 down, past the 27 July peak of £1.85m. Orders at 31,466 are effectively level with that peak, so the two weeks are structurally comparable.
2
Volume bought it, and basket paid for some of it. Orders +13.9% against sales +11.1%, with AOV down 2.5% to £59.09. Roughly a fifth of the volume gain went back in basket, which is expected under discounting but worth checking against margin.
3
Garmin is the one item to chase. −£10.0k total and −£10.5k NC, over a third of all decline in the week. Cirqa is live, so this is not stock — worth looking at traffic, placement and paid support on those listings.
4
LMNT fully recovered. +£23.4k total and +93.2% on NC, back above £115k. The split flagged a fortnight ago, where total grew while NC fell 31%, has closed on both measures.
5
Q4 gifting is stirring early. Our Place tripled to £10.9k, Therabody +52%, Hyperice +130%, Lumie up a third week. Four of the six Q4 categories moved in late August, which brings the stock conversation forward from September.
