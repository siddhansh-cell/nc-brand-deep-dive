NC Brand Sales Deep Dive

−21.1% WoW

w/c 3 Aug vs 10 Aug 2026 — New customer net sales by brand (UK e-com, excl. Healf Zone, HX, POS). Sharp, broad decline: new customers down 25.3% to 5,694, the lowest in the window. 106 of 137 tracked brands down. Garmin nearly doubles on the Cirqa launch and is the only material gain.

Last unpromoted week. The en-UK sale went live on 16 August, the final day of this week, with discounted prices still being uploaded across the top 750 SKUs that morning. So this week carried effectively no offer support, and the all-customer offer does not start until 21 August. Read the decline with that in mind, but note the customer-count fall is steeper than the sales fall, which offers alone would not explain.

Key takeaways

1
Acquisition volume, not basket. New customers fell 25.3% to 5,694, the lowest in the window and 31% below the 27 July peak. Sales per NC rose to £72.10, the highest in the window. The shortfall is entirely in how many people we converted.
2
This was the last unpromoted week. The en-UK sale went live on 16 August, the final day, with prices still loading that morning across the top 750 SKUs. The all-customer offer starts 21 August. Next week should show the lift, and will tell us how much of this was offer timing.
3
Garmin is the only material gain. +£6,952 and nearly doubling on the Cirqa launch, into the top 5 NC brands from outside the top 15 a fortnight ago. Together with Nurosym, HigherDOSE and Caraway it accounts for £15.2k of the £18.9k in total gains.
4
Tech turned as a block. WHOOP, Oura, COROS, Aarke and Hyperice all fell together for −£29.9k, having carried the previous two weeks. Aarke ends a four-week run. Balance Phone is the exception, holding flat at £9,581.
5
Thorne needs attention. Down 31.3% and a second heavy week, now £21.2k against £35.7k three weeks ago. The discontinued SKUs explained roughly half of the first fall and cannot explain this one.
