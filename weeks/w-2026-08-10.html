Key takeaways

1
Milder than it first looked. The week settled at −8.5% after late orders added about 16%, having initially read −21.1%. Worth treating any Monday pull of the immediately preceding week as provisional.
2
Volume, not basket — third week running. New customers −13.1% to 6,623 while sales per NC rose to £71.87, the highest in the window. Customer count is 20% below the 27 July peak. Against the 45k quarterly target this is the number that matters.
3
Garmin is carrying the upside. +£7,310 and more than doubling on the Cirqa launch, now the sixth largest NC brand. With Nurosym, HigherDOSE and Form Nutrition it offsets £17.0k of the £44.5k net decline.
4
Tech held up better than the average. COROS flat at +0.9%, Oura only −8.1% on its August retro, Balance Phone and Withings both up. Hyperice (−47.9%) and Aarke (−14.6%) are the exceptions, with Aarke ending a four-week run.
5
This was the last unpromoted week. The en-UK sale went live 16 August, the final day, and the all-customer offer follows on 21 August. Next week is the first with real offer support and the read on how much of this was timing.
