Key takeaways

1
A soft week, not a sharp one. Settled at −5.1% after late orders added about 16%, having initially read −17.9%. Orders −4.8% with AOV flat at £59, so nothing changed in what people bought.
2
The decline is concentrated, not broad. 248 brands up against 309 down of 561 trading. WHOOP and Thorne alone are £21.6k of the £84.6k fall, and LMNT actually grew 4.7%.
3
Garmin is the standout and it is real new demand. +£6,379 total but +£7,310 from new customers, so existing-customer sales fell. That is the signature of a genuine product launch rather than a promotion pulling repeat purchases forward.
4
Watch the LMNT split. Total sales up 4.7% while new-customer sales fell 31.3%. Existing customers more than covered it, but the acquisition side needs a look before the pattern sets.
5
NC share held at 30.3%. New and existing customers softened at similar rates, so this was not disproportionately an acquisition problem at the total level — the whole funnel was quieter ahead of the en-UK sale going live on 16 August.
