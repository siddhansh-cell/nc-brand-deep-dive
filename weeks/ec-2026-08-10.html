Key takeaways

1
Fewer orders, same basket. Sales −17.9% and orders −17.7% with AOV flat at £59. Nothing changed in what people bought, only how many bought. Two consecutive down weeks, now 26.6% off the 27 July peak.
2
This was the last unpromoted week. The en-UK sale went live 16 August, the final day, with prices still loading that morning. The all-customer offer follows on 21 August. Next week is the read on how much of this was offer timing.
3
The decline is in the core, not the tail. Thorne, Pure Encapsulations, WHOOP and Momentous alone are £75.6k of the £296k fall. 110 of 137 tracked brands were down.
4
Garmin is the only real gain, and it is genuinely new demand. +£5,837 total but +£6,952 from new customers, meaning existing-customer sales fell. That is the signature of a real product launch rather than a promotion pulling forward repeat purchases.
5
NC held its share at 30.2%. New customers fell faster than total sales in absolute terms, but the mix barely moved, so this was not disproportionately an acquisition problem — the whole funnel was quieter.
