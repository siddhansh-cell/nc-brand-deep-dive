Key takeaways

1
August is running 3.9% below the July daily average. £240.0k/day against £249.7k. Early July was a strong comparative, so the 6.1% like-for-like gap overstates the softening slightly.
2
Tech is carrying the month. WHOOP, Aarke, Oura, Omnilux, Garmin, Balance Phone and Withings added £61.5k. Without them the decline roughly doubles.
3
LMNT and Thorne are £44k of the £140k decline between them. Thorne is part-explained by discontinued SKUs; LMNT has no flagged cause and is the clearest thing to investigate.
4
Discontinued SKUs cost £24.6k across Seed, Organic Protein Company and Truvani. On all customers Seed alone is −£9.9k, versus −£1,859/week on NC — so the total-customer exposure is materially larger than the NC report shows.
5
Four brands in the decliner list have unresolved pricing flags — IM8, Green People, Biocare and Bare Biology, all down double digits with open match requests.
