Key takeaways

1
The volume trend has turned, and that is the important part. New customers +5.4% with basket flat, reversing three consecutive weeks of falling volume and rising basket. This is the first week with full offer support, so the offers look to be doing their job.
2
One week does not close the gap. At 6,975 customers we are still 15% below the 27 July peak of 8,250 and would need another two weeks at this rate to recover it. Against the 45k quarterly target, sustaining this matters more than the single week.
3
Seed is back. The short-expiry discontinuation has been reversed and the brand is at £6,818 total from £1,278, with NC returning from zero. That was the cleanest recovery on the discontinued-SKU list and it has landed.
4
Balance Phone is the standout brand story. +79.2% and into the top 5 NC brands, six consecutive weeks of growth since restocking. Alongside Omnilux resuming its ramp, these are the two new brands genuinely compounding.
5
Two to watch. COROS broke three flat weeks with a 20.9% fall and the pricing-threshold decision is still open. WHOOP is now three weeks down from £37.3k to £29.0k, which is a trend rather than noise. Meanwhile Lumie and Hatch are both ramping on schedule for Q4.
