Key takeaways

1
September: 28,687 new customers. 64% of target, and 5% fewer than September 2025. Total sales were +18% year on year, NC sales +2%, customer count −5%. We added £1.1m of revenue and acquired 1,600 fewer people. The weekly slide has stopped at 6,100 - up 66 on last week - but at that rate October lands around 27k. This is the number for the trade meeting.
2
We cut NC discounting 2.6pp and lost 1.4pp of margin. Discount rate 18.2% → 15.6%, product margin 32.9% → 31.5%. The volume shifted to WHOOP, Oura, COROS, Garmin and Aarke - tech and MAP brands - and away from supplements. That mix cost more than the discount cut saved. Retro-adjusted NC margin 31.9% is the lowest since the Healf Week baseline.
3
Wearables turned together, upward. WHOOP +20%, COROS +11%, Oura +10%, Garmin holding £14k. All four up on NC for the first time since August. Oura's rise came with retro cover cut from £4.6k to £1.7k - the first organic signal from Oura since the record. Last week's call that Oura "needs an owner" was two weeks early; watch it rather than act.
4
Organic Protein Company has topped the lost-revenue view for ten weeks. £10.6k a week in June, £1.1k now, discount collapsed to 3% because there is nothing left to discount. Roughly £95k of Q3 sales not taken. It needs a named owner and a date, not another week on the list. SAP Good Energy (units 296 → 30) and Lola (back to 15 units) are the other two hard stock-outs this week.
5
Half the lost-revenue brands grew anyway. COROS (+12%), Pure Encapsulations (+9%), NOW Foods (+12%), Hooga (+118%), NodPod (+68%) and Gato are all on the Top 50 view and all rose. The view ranks modelled loss; it does not distinguish a brand short on three SKUs from a brand with an empty shelf. Worth adding a "sales this week" column so the list prioritises itself.
6
Two corrections. The Ultimate Human's two-week doubling was the 33–42% discount - it unwound 64% the week the discount eased and retro ended. Garmin's £29.6k all-customer spike was £14.4k of existing-customer sales in one week, not stock landing; NC held at £14k, which is the real and healthy level.
7
Q4 gifting is live. Hatch doubled to £8.6k NC, its best week by a distance; NodPod +108%, Caraway +96%, Aarke +77%. Lola and Black+Blum fell but both are stock-bound. Eight weeks to Black Friday and three of the gifting brands are short.
