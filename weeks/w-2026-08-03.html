Key takeaways

1
Volume, not basket. Sales down 2.9% but new customers down 7.6%, with net sales per NC at £68.31, the highest in the window. Against the 45k quarterly target the count is the number that matters.
2
Tech held the week up. Oura, Garmin, WHOOP, Aarke, Hyperice and Balance Phone added £20.5k between them. Oura's August retro is the biggest single gain and Garmin's new Cirqa launch the second.
3
Metagenics shows price is the lever. Retro drove +156%, then −48.6% once it ended, settling 32% above the pre-retro base. Being cheapest in the market is what moved the brand.
4
Discontinued SKUs cost £6.3k of NC a week, ~£330k annualised. Seed is at zero on a single short-expiry tag worth £1,859/week with three conflicting explanations. Organic Protein Company is partially back, so retrievable.
5
Back Omnilux and Balance Phone. The only two new brands compounding — Omnilux £508 to £5,134 over four weeks. Six others gave back £10.8k after one strong week each.
