Key takeaways

1
Existing customers came back; new customers did not. Total +5.2% on existing +8.2%, with retros down 68% - organic repeat demand returning after Healf Week. NC −2.2%, and the customer count fell for a fourth straight week to 6,037, a new low. 1–27 Sep has delivered roughly 25.7k new customers; the month will land near 28.5k against 45k.
2
Oura is the biggest single NC problem in the book. £44.1k → £17.6k over four weeks with retro cover held throughout, so it is not the retro. Units −52%. WHOOP is at a window low too, while Garmin, COROS and Withings all grew - the subscription wearables are falling and the watches are not. Needs an owner and a D2C price check.
3
Garmin is back at its August level - and swinging with stock. NC £15.1k, all-customer £29.6k, the best week in the window, on a discount rate of 1.95% - people are paying full price. But it has gone £2.5k → £11.8k → £4.1k → £15.1k over five weeks, which is what intermittent stock looks like, not a demand trend. No launch or restock message in Slack this month. A lost revenue extract would settle it; if stock is the constraint, this is the easiest £10k a week on the page to make permanent.
4
Healf Week's retro scorecard: two held, six did not. Of the brands whose cover went to zero, BodyHealth (+21% vs pre-retro) and Energybits held their gains. Promix, JENKI and Metagenics are back to base. Dream Recovery, Wild Nutrition and BON CHARGE are below it - BON CHARGE 38% below. £94k of cover bought two durable lifts.
5
Two corrections to last week. LMNT: the 4for3 code was never removed, only dipped for the test cohort, and LMNT recovered +20% all-customer this week - the low was a one-week dip, though the −44% since July stands. The Ultimate Human: its doubling came with a 41.6% discount rate, not "without price" as I said. It held this week at 33.5%. Real volume, expensive volume.
6
Margin has found its unpromoted level. NC retro-adjusted 34.04%, total 37.77%, with only £10.9k of retro left. Product margin before retros held at 37.08%. That is the baseline to judge October against.
7
Q4 gifting: Black+Blum and Lola moved, Lumie and NodPod are stock-bound. Black+Blum +257% to its best week with its first £565 of cover; Lola +132% as stock landed. Lumie gave back last week's recovery and NodPod halved, with Body Sage and four mask colours at zero on 26 Sep. Five weeks to Black Friday.
