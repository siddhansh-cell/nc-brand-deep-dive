Key takeaways

1
A genuinely good week on the existing book. +4.4% to £1.66m with orders +9.2%, discount rate cut to 10.6% (window low), retros £3.3k, product margin 37.45% (window high). Strip out the two spike unwinds and the book was +7.9%. Thorne £147k is the largest brand-week in the window. The supplements range moved together on payday at a lower discount than the week before. That is earned growth.
2
September closed at 28,687 new customers - 64% of target and 5% below last September. Sales +18% YoY, NC sales +2%, new customers −5%. We added £1.1m of revenue and acquired 1,643 fewer people. The weekly slide stopped this week (6,036 → 6,102) but October at this rate lands near 27k. This is the trade meeting number.
3
The lost-revenue view needs a sales column. Of its top blocks, COROS, Pure Encapsulations, NOW Foods, Nordic Naturals, Hooga, WHOOP, MaryRuth's and DoNotAge all grew this week - short on SKUs inside ranges that are selling. The three where the shelf is actually empty are Organic Protein Company (ten weeks, £95k of Q3 not taken), SAP Good Energy (units 296 → 30) and Lola Blankets (back to 15 units, Q4 brand). Those three need owners and dates.
4
Wearables turned together, upward. WHOOP +13%, COROS +12%, Oura flat at £26.2k with retro cover cut 63% - its first non-decline in five weeks. Garmin NC held at £14k; the £29.6k all-customer last week was a one-off existing order, not stock landing. Last week's call that Oura needs an owner was premature. Watch, do not act.
5
The Ultimate Human spike was the discount. £32.3k → £16.7k the week the discount rate eased from 33.5% to 28.7% and retro went to £0. Two weeks of window highs bought at 33–42% off, then gone. Settles the question from a fortnight ago.
6
The weekend trough is four weeks old. Fri–Sun £608k vs £688k the week before; Friday 716 new customers is the lowest day in the window. Tue–Thu added £152k, Fri–Sun lost £79k. Four consecutive weeks of the same shape is a pacing question for Leo.
7
Q4 gifting is live and three brands are short. Hatch +95% to a window high at £11.2k, Aarke +58%, NodPod, Caraway and Therabody all up. Lola and Black+Blum fell - both stock-bound. Eight weeks to Black Friday.
