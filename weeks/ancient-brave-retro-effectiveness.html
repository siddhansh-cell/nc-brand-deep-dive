Margin shown is product margin. Where a rebate was received, the arrow shows product margin → retro-adjusted margin. Extended to four months because the key comparison week falls in May.
The retro barely registered+4.1% sales, £813 rebate
Two retro weeks produced £11.3k a week against a £10.9k baseline. That is inside normal weekly noise — the brand did £11.4k the week after the retro ended with no support at all. Total rebate income was £813, against £4,558 for Metagenics over the same period.

The margin mechanic worked exactly as it should: product margin fell to 37.8% and the rebate lifted it back to 42.2%. We were protected. There simply was not any volume to protect.
Why — all five hero jars were excluded
The promo terms carved out True Collagen Jar, Wild Collagen Jar, Creatine Jar, Hydration Jar and Nightcap Jar. Sachets only. So the 20% applied to the peripheral range while the hero products stayed at full price.

The counter-evidence is in the same dataset. The best week in four months was 25 May at £15.2k, up 40% on baseline — a self-funded discount with a 16.7% discount rate and no exclusions. Ancient + Brave clearly does respond to price. It just does not respond to a discount on the products people are not coming for.
And there is margin headroom to do it properly43.5% baseline
Ancient + Brave runs at 43.5% product margin, higher than Metagenics at 39.8%, and it held at 42–44% every single week including through the discounted ones. This is not a brand where the jars cannot take a discount on our side. The exclusion was a brand-side condition, not a margin constraint.

The pattern across both brands
Retro depth and funding split are not what determines the outcome. Scope and visibility are.

The best-funded retro we ran all year was Metagenics at 25% fully funded, and it produced nothing because the code was hidden. Ancient + Brave got the same 20% cofunded terms as the Metagenics retro that drove +45%, and produced +4% because the hero range was carved out.

One deal shape worked: visible, on-site, no hero exclusions. That is the term to negotiate for, ahead of percentage.
