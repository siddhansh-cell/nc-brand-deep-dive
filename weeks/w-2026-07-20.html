SURI−£7,756 (−57.5%)
Price played the role. We were leading on price on the Monday, then reverted that morning because Boots had stopped discounting and the brand was pushing back. John Lewis followed us down as soon as we reverted, and we spent the rest of the week uncompetitive — SURI tracks price about as tightly as any brand we carry. Reverted again on 24 Jul. The brand partner team is negotiating exclusive retros in exchange for taking SURI into the EU, which would remove the need to keep managing price against other retailers week to week.
Oura Ring−£6,677 (−23.1%)Retro 01-31 Aug
Second consecutive week down and the largest OOS exposure in the business. Still holding RRP with the brand relationship sensitive. The August retro should lift volume but will not fix the stock position.
Therabody−£3,575 (−54.3%)Post-retro
July rolling retro has come off. New retros already loaded for 11-31 Aug and into September, so expect soft through early August then recovery from mid-month.
WHOOP−£4,019 (−13.7%)
Gives back last week's gain and returns to its early-July level. Tech was split this week — WHOOP, Oura and Hyperice down, Withings and Garmin up — so volatility rather than share loss.
Hyperice−£1,785 (−21.6%)Post-retro
Second week of post-retro decline, now half its £13.2k peak. The payback is running longer than one week — worth factoring into how we forecast the tail on high-ticket retros.
HigherDOSE−£1,855 (−36.5%)
Falls back after last week's restocking spike. Still well above the £965 trough, so the restock worked — the spike was pent-up demand clearing, not a new run-rate.
Green People−£756 (−52.1%)
Third straight week down. Comps are pricing below what we can match on Hydrating After Sun 200ml and Scent Free Sun Cream SPF30, both flagged in July and neither resolved. Seasonal window closing while we are uncompetitive on the two SKUs that matter.
BioCare−£682 (−17.0%)
Third consecutive week down. Match permission covers the brand's defined top 10 SKUs only and we must never undercut. The pricing team has asked to extend cover to the top 15 because the SKUs cycling in and out of the top 10 keep leaving gaps — that request is with the brand.
NOW Foods−£964 (−20.8%)
Third week of decline with OOS spread thinly across 35 SKUs. Breadth rather than depth — points at replenishment cadence rather than a single supply failure.

Key takeaways

1
SURI is the clearest price-elasticity read we have. We led on price on the Monday, reverted that morning, John Lewis followed us down, and the brand halved (−£7.8k) over the rest of the week with no stock issue. Reverted again on 24 Jul. The exclusive-retro-for-EU negotiation is the structural fix; until then this brand needs watching daily.
2
New brands carried the week. The cohort delivered £28.6k, 5.7% of NC sales, and added £14.1k of the £17.5k total increase. Omnilux, NIRA, Fittle and CELLER8 drove the step up. Almost all of this week's growth came from recently added brands rather than the core.
3
Two of the top three movers are noise, not trend. Bala's +£4.6k is a single large order, and the LYMA / Fittle / CELLER8 / FÔLD swing of +£8.0k is lumpy high-ticket ordering off a zero week. Strip both out and the underlying week is closer to flat.
4
Retro timing explains most of the top-20 decline. Therabody (−54%) and Hyperice (−22%, second week) are both post-retro, together −£5.4k. Therabody's next retro is loaded for 11 Aug and Oura's for 1 Aug, so August should recover both. BON CHARGE is the counter-example — holding flat on an active retro.
5
The recovery is real but partial. +3.6% to £505k with 249 brands up against 241 down, still ~5% below the £531k June run-rate. Margin improved +0.24pp to 34.15%. Oura Ring remains the single largest drag on both sales and stock availability.
