Key takeaways

1
The recovery is real and nearly complete. £74.6k/day is within 1.6% of the June average, after two consecutive weeks of gains from the £69.6k trough. 271 brands up against 220 down. Ignore the −11.3% raw number — that is the missing seventh day.
2
SURI closes the loop on price elasticity. Reverted price, halved. Went back to matching, +45%. Two interventions and two clean responses in consecutive weeks is about as strong a read as weekly data gives you. The case for the exclusive-retro-for-EU deal is now evidenced rather than argued.
3
Price is making and breaking the movers, not demand. SURI recovered on matching, H2Tab broke a three-week run when a comp discounted, Maurten fell back once the day-one launch discount was pulled, and Kiki improved when resellers were cut off. Four of this week's biggest swings are pricing decisions, and three of them are still open in #discounting-issues.
4
Retro payback is consistent enough to plan around. BON CHARGE joins Therabody and Hyperice in giving the volume straight back once the retro ends. Across all three the post-retro week lands near the pre-retro level, so the true incremental is well below the in-retro uplift. Oura's August retro went live 1 Aug and will be the next test.
5
COROS is the one to actually dig into. Four straight weeks down and now the largest core decline, with MAP blocking any price response. Everything else in the decliner list is explained — retro timing, order lumpiness, or a known pricing flag. COROS is not.
