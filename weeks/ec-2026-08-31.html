Key takeaways

1
£1.68m off a record, with the margin back. Sales −9.5% but product margin +1.40pp to 36.13% and retro-adjusted +1.05pp to 37.01%. Two weeks below 35% have reversed. The trade meeting question is whether the £176.9k was worth the 1.4pp.
2
The Monday sale was an existing-customer event, not an acquisition one. Existing customers are 79% of the decline. Monday 24 August did £319k, the highest day of the fortnight, and alone explains 36% of the fall - yet NC sales were higher on the following Monday. If the objective is new customers, a 12-hour sitewide sale is not the mechanic.
3
Volume fell, basket did not. Orders −9.7%, units −9.8%, AOV +0.2%. Discount rate actually rose slightly to 12.44% of gross. So the margin gain came from mix and retro timing rather than from discounting less, which means it should hold.
4
Retro cover moved £7.9k and the brands followed it. Retros fell 35% to £14,532. Seven brands lost or reduced cover and six of them declined - HigherDOSE, BodyBio, Therabody, Nurosym, Nuzest and Withings are −£27.1k between them, with The Ultimate Human the one exception. BON CHARGE gained cover from £0 and rose 44%; SURI's cover rose 2.4x and it rose 16%; Oura held cover and hit its best week. The lever is proven and under-supplied.
5
£100k of lost revenue, all of it recoverable. 584 live SKUs at zero stock across 192 brand rows, with bundled, dropship and discontinued lines already excluded - so this is availability, not range decisions. Momentous is two thirds explained by it (£6,373 against a £9,669 decline, 21 SKUs down), Lumie more than fully explained (£4,783 against a £2,234 decline), and Garmin is losing more than it sells. BON CHARGE grew 44% while carrying £4,810 on three SKUs, which is the cheapest upside on this page.
6
LMNT is the single biggest problem in the book. Down £27.1k, 15% of the entire decline, and 945 units lighter. NC has drifted from £17.2k in late July to £12.4k. Discount rate is broadly unchanged, so price is not the answer. This needs an owner.
7
Q4 gifting continues to move early, and short. Hatch +33%, Black+Blum +85%, plus Caraway and Lola Blankets doubling on NC. Our Place is holding 2.4x its July base since the restock Lestat pushed for on 24 August. But Lumie, Lola Blankets, NodPod and Hatch are losing £10,390 to stock right now. Second consecutive week of Q4 lift and we are already short on the list - stock cover needs checking this week.
