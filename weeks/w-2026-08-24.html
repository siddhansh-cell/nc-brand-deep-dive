Key takeaways

1
Record NC week at £551.7k, past the July peak. Two consecutive weeks of volume-led growth have recovered the whole August dip. Customers +11.2% to 7,753, now within 500 of the 8,250 high. Against the 45k quarterly target this is the trajectory you want.
2
Garmin needs attention. NC has gone from £14,286 to £1,018 in two weeks and accounts for 76% of all NC decline this week. Cirqa is live, so this is not stock. A brand that more than doubled a month ago falling 91% in a record week needs a cause — traffic, placement or paid support are the places to look.
3
LMNT's acquisition problem has resolved. NC +93.2% to £20,985, above its pre-dip level. The split flagged two weeks ago, where total sales grew while NC fell 31%, has closed.
4
Tech carried the week, in both directions. Oura (+£12.9k, now the largest NC brand), Hyperice (+£9.1k, tripling), COROS (+£3.0k) and WHOOP (+£2.7k) added £27.7k, while Garmin lost £10.5k. Oura's August retro produced its strongest week of the month.
5
Q4 gifting brands are moving three weeks early. Our Place +248%, Loop +85%, Lumie +21% for a third straight week. Three of the six Q4 categories are already lifting in late August, which brings the stock-timing question forward.
