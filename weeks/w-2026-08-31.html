NC Brand Sales Deep Dive

−6.6% WoW

w/c 24 Aug vs 31 Aug 2026 — New customer net sales by brand (UK e-com, excl. Healf Zone, HX, POS). Off the record week to £513.3k, still the third best week in the window. Post-promotion week, and the promo was an EC event. The 12-hour Monday sale on 24 August drove £319k in a single day, the highest of the fortnight. Key takeaways

1
A soft landing off a record, and the margin came back. NC fell 6.6% to £513.3k, still the third best week in the window and above every week in the first half of August. NC product margin up 1.22pp to 30.19%, total up 1.40pp to 36.13%. The record was built on a 12-hour Monday sale that drove £319k in a day, so some give-back was mechanical - the question is whether the £176.9k was worth the 1.4pp.
2
£100k of lost revenue is the biggest single lever on the page. 584 live SKUs at zero stock across 192 brand rows, with bundled, dropship and discontinued lines already excluded - so all of it is recoverable. Set against a £176.9k sales decline, that is not a rounding error. Four of the five brands flagged are in the top seven: Organic Protein Company, Momentous, BON CHARGE, Lumie and Garmin are £20,702 on 40 SKUs, across three different ops owners.
3
Organic Protein Company is the worst case in the book. NC has gone from a £2,710 peak on 6 July to £50, down 98.2%, and the brand is now losing £7,028 a week to stock against £1,804 of actual sales - 3.9x. It was doubling into July before 208 units became 8 in a single week. Six weeks at this level is roughly £13k of NC run rate gone, and none of it is a range decision.
4
Garmin has an answer at last. Reports 15 to 17 carried the Garmin fall as unexplained. Six SKUs sit at zero inventory carrying £4,736 of lost revenue against £3,732 of sales - it is losing more than it sells. The Cirqa listing being live was never the whole picture.
5
Retro cover explains 44% of the NC fall, and it works in both directions. Seven of the eight brands that lost or reduced cover declined, −£16.0k between them. BON CHARGE went from £0 to £3,243 of cover and NC more than doubled - while carrying £4,810 of lost revenue on three SKUs, so fixing stock would have taken it further still. SURI's cover rose 2.4x and NC rose 24.8%; Oura held cover and posted its best NC week on record.
6
Ancient + Brave cannot respond to a retro that excludes 60% of it. The excluded heroes are 59.3% of the brand on the promo-named SKUs and 62.7% including their subscription and bundle variants. True Collagen Powder alone is 39.2%. Availability is not the issue - A+B has £168 of lost revenue on one SKU and grew 3.4% this week. The promoted range over-performed where it was allowed to (11.2% vs a 9.5% neutral share), and we hold 43.5% margin, so there is headroom to fund our half. Next window needs the jars in scope.
7
The promo was an existing-customer event. Existing customers are 79% of the total £176.9k decline while NC is only 21%. NC was actually up £2.7k on the Monday itself and NC share of the business rose to 30.6%. Worth knowing before we design the next one.
8
LMNT needs a plan. NC averaged £16.5k in the four weeks to 3 Aug and £11.6k across the three non-promo weeks since. All-customer is down £27.1k, the largest decline in the business. Two promo spikes have masked the step down. Discount rate is broadly unchanged and stock is not the issue, so this is demand.
9
Q4 gifting is lifting early and going into peak short. Hatch +43.7% to its best week in eight, Caraway +108%, Lola Blankets +192%, Black+Blum +175%. But Lumie is losing £4,783, Lola Blankets £2,262, NodPod £2,576 and Hatch £769 to stock right now. These are exactly the brands we cannot be out of in November - Q4 stock cover should be checked this week, not in October.
